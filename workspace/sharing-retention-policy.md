## 1. Where files live
All active business documents and spreadsheets reside in designated Google Workspace Shared Drives managed by the practice OU. Personal employee storage is prohibited for core operational files.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is strictly restricted. Files can only be shared with pre-approved external partner domains; public link sharing is disabled across all organizational units.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active personnel authenticated via corporate credentials with mandatory 2-Step Verification (2SV) enforced may access the Google Workspace domain. Unauthorized sign-in attempts are blocked and logged.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Financial and operational records are retained for 7 years in secure administrative archives. Employee correspondence and temporary drafts are purged after 12 months.

## 5. The monthly export
Administrators execute a full monthly export of directory data, Drive audit logs, and user archives, storing encrypted copies in offline backup storage.

## 6. Who holds admin
Super-admin privileges are held exclusively by designated senior administrators with hardware-backed security keys. Least-privilege roles are assigned for daily management tasks.

## 7. When someone leaves
When an employee departs, their account is immediately suspended, Drive file ownership is transferred to the designated manager via the Admin Console, and a final Takeout export is archived before account deletion.
